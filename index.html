Research

My research interests are in mathematical and computational linguistics, especially in subregular morphophonology and string-to-string function decomposition problems. My current work considers different models of UR assignment. I also work on the learning of tiers, often with Vincent Czarnecki.

keywords: mathematical linguistics; formal language theory; subregular linguistics; phonology; formal grammar; automata
